# 旅行行为分析


## 旅行前

### 当前旅行规划方式

主要通过小红书搜索旅行攻略。


典型流程：


产生旅行想法

↓

搜索小红书攻略

↓

查看别人旅行方案

↓

自己整理路线

↓

预订交通和住宿



### 主要痛点

- 不确定哪些攻略真实可靠
- 需要花时间筛选参考方案
- 不知道如何安排更合理的路线
- 住宿选择困难


---

## 旅行中


### 常用工具

- 地图导航


### 遇到的问题

- 曾经出现迷路情况
- 遇到过攻略信息不准确的问题


### 用户需求

希望旅行过程中能够快速获得可靠的信息和建议。


---

## 旅行后


### 当前习惯

会整理照片。


### 希望保存的旅行记忆

- 购买的纪念品
- 旅行中的特殊经历


### 特点

不会主动写长篇旅行日记，更倾向于简单保存旅行回忆。

# User Persona

## Research Background

This persona document is based on real user interviews with three university students and young independent travelers.

Research participants:

- 面条
- 乌柩
- 芊沫

Research goal:

Understand users' travel behaviors, pain points and expectations for AI travel assistants.

---

# Persona 01：面条

## Basic Information

Name:

面条


User Type:

University Student Traveler


Travel Frequency:

1-2 times per year


Travel Style:

Free independent travel


Travel Budget:

Depends on destination


Common Travel Platforms:

- Xiaohongshu
- Travel booking platforms


---

## Travel Behavior


### Before Travel

Typical planning process:

Search travel guides on Xiaohongshu and compare information.


Planning process:
Generate travel idea

↓

Search Xiaohongshu

↓

Collect guides

↓

Plan itinerary

↓

Book transportation and accommodation



Main problems:

- Hard to find reliable travel plans
- Needs to manually compare different guides
- Difficult to decide where to stay


---

### During Travel

Common tools:

- Navigation applications


Problems:

- Has experienced getting lost
- Has encountered outdated travel information


Needs:

Quick access to practical travel suggestions.


---

### After Travel

Memory habits:

Will organize photos.


Preferred memories:

- Souvenirs
- Interesting travel experiences


Does not usually write detailed travel diaries.


---

## AI Travel Assistant Expectations


Most valuable function:

> Provide realistic and referenceable travel plans.


Expected AI capabilities:

- Generate practical itineraries
- Recommend suitable routes
- Reduce planning time


---

## Key Insight

面条 represents users who have travel needs but lack professional planning experience.

The main value of AI for this user is:

"Help me quickly get a reliable travel plan."

# Overall Persona Insights

Based on three interviews:


## Common Characteristics

All users:

- Prefer independent travel
- Use Xiaohongshu for travel research
- Spend time planning routes and accommodation
- Want practical travel suggestions


---

## Main Pain Points


### Before Travel

Highest priority:

1. Route planning difficulty

2. Accommodation selection difficulty

3. Information scattered across platforms


### During Travel

1. Navigation problems

2. Outdated information


### After Travel

1. Travel memories are not organized
